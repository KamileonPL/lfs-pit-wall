Quick Guide

What changes usually do

More front grip

Usually comes from softer front bar, more front camber, lower front tyre pressure or more front wing.

More rear traction

Often means softer rear bar, calmer diff locking, less aggressive rear damping or lower rear tyre pressure.

Better braking stability

Usually more front brake bias, less rear lock tendency and a setup that does not overload the rear axle.

More top speed

Usually lower wing, longer final drive and gearing, but watch acceleration and corner exit performance.
